About

Architect, delivery lead and trusted technical advisor

I am a Solutions Architect at Sagesoft Solutions Inc. I also lead the Service Delivery tech team and act as Technical Account Manager (TAM) for client accounts, so I own both the design and the day-to-day outcome.

Government WordPress to AWS Auto Scaling

Replatforming a public government WordPress site.
Problem
The site ran on a legacy server with an end-of-life operating system.
Approach
Staging and Production in separate VPCs, an Auto Scaling Group behind an ALB, RDS for the database, and a phased cutover plan.
Outcome
A documented, repeatable plan with a rollback path.

  • ALB
  • RDS
  • Route 53
  • WordPress

Services

What I bring to a project

AWS Architecture Design

Scalable, secure, cost-optimized architectures using the Well-Architected Framework.

End-to-end AWS architectures tailored to the workload: VPC networking, compute sizing, database selection and security controls. Each design comes with architecture diagrams and AWS Calculator estimates, across all five Well-Architected pillars.

Cloud Migration

Moving legacy or on-premise systems to AWS with minimal downtime.

Discovery, assessment, execution and optimization, whether lift-and-shift, replatform or re-architecture, with testing and rollback strategies. Experience with monoliths to containers, databases to RDS and websites to S3/CloudFront.

DevOps and CI/CD

Automated deployments and infrastructure.

CI/CD pipelines, Docker, CloudFormation and Terraform. Blue-green deployments, automated backups and scheduled scaling.

Security and Compliance

WAF, VAPT remediation, governance controls.

AWS WAF, Shield, Security Hub, GuardDuty and IAM practices, VAPT remediation and SSL/TLS management. For many accounts: AWS Organizations with SCPs and Control Tower.

Backup and Disaster Recovery

Backup strategy and recovery plans.

Backup strategies for EC2, RDS and ElastiCache with scheduling and retention, and cross-region replication and multi-AZ for critical workloads. Recovery procedures are tested.

Web Development

Websites and portals on modern CMS platforms.

Corporate sites, eCommerce stores, event platforms and web portals, plus domain, SSL, hosting and ongoing maintenance.

Client Training and Knowledge Transfer

Hands-on AWS sessions and documentation.

Training on AWS fundamentals, Auto Scaling, storage, databases and security with real-environment labs, plus documentation, architecture rationale and runbooks.
