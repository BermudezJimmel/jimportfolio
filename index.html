About

Architect, delivery lead and trusted technical advisor

I am a Solutions Architect at Sagesoft Solutions Inc. I also lead the Service Delivery tech team and act as Technical Account Manager (TAM) for client accounts, so I own both the design and the day-to-day outcome.

My work covers multi-account governance with AWS Control Tower, migrations and replatforming, WAF assessments, infrastructure as code with Terraform, CI/CD, and hands-on training for client teams. I also build with Amazon Bedrock and Kiro.
